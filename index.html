How to use it

A finishing oil, first

Great olive oil is a finishing move. Save the cheap stuff for the pan — reach for Nicholas at the end.

Drizzle

Over warm bread, burrata, roasted vegetables, or a bowl of soup — the finishing pour that wakes a dish up.

Dip

Crusty bread, a little flaky salt, cracked pepper. The simplest way to taste how alive fresh oil really is.

Dress

Greens, grains, tomatoes at their peak. A splash of Nicholas, lemon, salt — and nothing else needed.
